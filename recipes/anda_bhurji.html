Anda Bhurji

Back to Homepage

Ingredients

  • Chilli, Coriander, Haldi Powder and Salt
  • Fine chopped onions, tomatoes and green chilli
  • Egg Yolk
  • Cooking Oil

Instructions

  1. Prepare the ingredients before hand.
  2. Put some oil in the kadhai, and let it heat.
  3. Add freshly chopped onions and fry them.
  4. Add tomatoes and fry them alongside the onions.
  5. Add the prepared masala powders.
  6. Add the Egg Yolk.
  7. Keep stirring till it assumes the shape of the anda bhurji.
